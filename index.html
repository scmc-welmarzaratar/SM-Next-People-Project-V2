from support to ownership.

This project will help my subordinates become more confident and capable owners of assigned RUN operations and CHANGE-stage work because they will receive clear responsibilities, defined decision and escalation boundaries, focused coaching, and opportunities to demonstrate readiness through actual work. It will strengthen their initiative, accountability, communication, collaboration, and ability to sustain the RPA portfolio with less avoidable dependence on manager intervention.

NEXT · Success measures
05 · SUCCESS MEASURES

Proposed targets, not achieved results.

100%Mapped primary + backup
≥95%Sampled monitoring completion
≥90%Within priority response target
≥85%Readiness, ≥17/20
≥25%Avoidable takeover rate reduction

KPI basis Every intervention is classified as planned coaching, required approval, appropriate escalation or avoidable takeover. Only the last category enters this KPI.

Baseline Week 1 rate = avoidable takeovers ÷ comparable assigned work items. Week 4 uses the same case types and denominator rule.

Decision rule Target ≥25% rate reduction. If Week 1 has fewer than 4 avoidable events, report counts and evidence only, not percentage improvement.

Targets are decision gates, not claims of achieved results.

NEXT · Appendix: takeover rule
APPENDIX B · MANAGER INVOLVEMENT CLASSIFICATION

Separate healthy support from ownership gaps.

Planned coachingEXCLUDE

Scheduled guidance, feedback or observation without taking ownership away.

Required approvalEXCLUDE

Decision reserved for management by policy, materiality, compliance or authority limit.

Appropriate escalationEXCLUDE

Staff identifies a risk, follows the agreed path and retains coordination ownership.

Avoidable takeoverCOUNT

Manager performs, redirects, completes or closes in-scope work because of an ownership, follow-up, communication, execution or escalation gap.

Takeover rate = avoidable takeovers ÷ comparable assigned work items
Reduction % = (Week 1 rate − Week 4 rate) ÷ Week 1 rate × 100
Same case types in Weeks 1 and 4Record reason + evidenceManager validates disputesIf baseline <4 events, report counts only
Count only preventable ownership gaps, not healthy coaching, required approvals or correct escalations.
